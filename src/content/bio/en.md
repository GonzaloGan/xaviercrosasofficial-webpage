My journey started in 2020, when invited by curiosity about the hero's journey, a book called
'The Hero Within' by Carol S. Pearson came into my hands. Through this reading, my inspiration to
compose about it was born. Sometime later, I had the chance to start a new life in a foreign
country. Before leaving, one of my mentors gave me a book of his authorship called
'Crisis: ¿Estás preparado para crecer?'
